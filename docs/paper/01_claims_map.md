## Column definitions

- **level** — KNOWLEDGE's own level word where it gives one; otherwise §1a's vocabulary (exact /
  solver-anchored / search-relative / predictive / behavioural / descriptive / decodability /
  supervised / single run against a named parent). Where the two differ, KNOWLEDGE's word is given
  first and §1a's in parentheses.
- **coverage** — the actual checkpoints, seeds, architectures and corpora, from the claim's *held
  across* clause and its file names. Never a category: "all four strong nets" does not appear.
- **drift** — the verdict at the **last re-read**: *compatible* (inside the earlier interval — a
  descriptive rule, not an equivalence test) / *magnitude changed* / *sign or order changed* /
  *unresolved* / *not measured*. Every E-level row carries one. Every *compatible* cell is marked
  **[CI]** where an earlier interval actually carries the verdict, or **[no CI]** where there is
  **no interval-based drift comparison** — a repeated rank, share or profile, or match intervals
  quoted elsewhere in the row that never test the drift (broadened from "no interval was ever
  quoted"; M1 rebuttal, 2026-09-13). Five rows in the *compatible* bucket carry an interval-based
  verdict at their last re-read — **8, 13, 19, 35 (regression), 45** — and three further
  interval-based *compatible* components sit inside compound cells (**9, 14, 16**). Row 44 was
  marked **[CI]** until the rebuttal and is not: its gains over deep10 put each later estimate
  outside its predecessor's interval.
- **strength columns** — `v2b` = `v2b/net_0150.pt` (0 Elo); `deep8_300` = `deep8_c1_300` (+211);
  `deep10` = `deep10_c1_300` (+242); `s1` = `deep10_c1_300_s1` (+213); `_e4` = `deep8_c1_300_e4`
  (+363, the net §1–§8 were re-read on); `_e8` = `deep8_c1_300_e8` (+40 over `_e4`, the play agent).
  For 42–51 a column carries that claim's reading *against* that net, and the strength it "held at" is
  the parent's, named in the coverage cell. dev1 and v2a are folded into the tool cell.
